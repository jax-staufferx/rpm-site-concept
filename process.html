  1. 01 · Review

    We review your drawing before we quote it

    Every request gets a manufacturability and contract review first. We check the drawing and model, the material spec, any special processes, what inspection and paperwork you need, and what the parts are for.

    If something on your print is a problem, such as a callout we can't measure, we raise it when we quote, not after the parts are made.

    • The quote lists every process and outside operation
    • Parts are for bench testing, preclinical studies or design verification
  2. 02 · Machine

    Swiss turning and CNC milling

    On a Swiss-type lathe, the bar slides out past a tool that cuts right where the bar is supported. That keeps long, slender parts like screws, pins and tubes rigid while they're turned, and deep holes can be drilled on the same machine. The finished part is cut off the bar complete.

    Plates and other prismatic parts go to our CNC mill. The first part off every setup is checked before the rest are made.

    • Kyle Walker runs Swiss turning
    • Alex Aspinwall runs CNC milling
  3. 03 · Finish

    Cleaning, blasting and passivation

    Finishing happens in the same building as the machines, in this order: an ultrasonic clean, bead or oxide blasting one part at a time, a second clean, then citric passivation for stainless parts. Blast media for stainless is kept separate.

    Anodizing, laser marking, laser welding, wire EDM and nitric passivation go to partners. We set the order on our purchase order, inspect the parts when they come back and reference the partner's certification, so you still deal with one shop.

  4. 04 · Inspect

    Every part is inspected on orders under ten

    For prototypes, test parts and lots under ten, we check every characteristic on the drawing, on every piece. A new part number gets a first article with every dimension measured and recorded, and Quality accepts it before the rest are made.

    We write down the actual measurement for each dimension, not just a pass or fail.

  5. 05 · Assemble & ship

    Packing, paperwork and shipping

    Multi-part instruments are assembled in-house. Every shipment is labelled and goes out with a Certificate of Conformance listing the material and lot, every special process and who performed it, attached certifications, and any exceptions.

    If you need inspection data, material certs or a first article report in your own format, tell us when you ask for a quote and it ships with the parts. We keep the records behind every certificate for at least five years.
