Quantities

We make prototype quantities, starting at one part.

Prototypes, first articles and test parts are most of what we do. Here's how an order is handled depending on its size.

1 to 9 pieces

  • Every characteristic on the drawing inspected, on every part
  • First article with every dimension recorded, for a new part number
  • Certificate of Conformance with the shipment

10 pieces and up

  • Critical characteristics checked on every part; the rest sampled to a standard plan, or 100% if your print calls for it
  • First article before the run, for a new part number
  • Certificate of Conformance with every shipment

Need more than a small lot? Tell us what you need and we'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.

Capabilities

What we do in‑house, and what we send to partners

Eight processes happen in our building. Four go to partners we manage, so you still deal with one shop.

In-house, Arcadia IN

  • 01

    Engineering & DFM review

    Drawing, material and process review before any quote.

  • 02

    Swiss turning

    Screws, pins, tubes and slender parts, including deep-hole drilling.

  • 03

    CNC milling

    Plates, locks and prismatic parts from bar.

  • 04

    Blast finishing

    Bead and oxide media, one part at a time, media kept separate for stainless.

  • 05

    Ultrasonic cleaning

    Two-stage clean, before and after blasting.

  • 06

    Citric passivation

    For stainless parts. Nitric or a named spec through partners.

  • 07

    Heat treat

    To the condition your material spec requires.

  • 08

    Assembly

    Press-fit and multi-part instrument assembly.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a finished drawing to contact you?

No. Call or send a note with whatever you have, even just an idea. If it turns into a quote, we'll tell you exactly what we need.

Will you quote a single part?

Yes. Prototypes, first articles and test parts are most of our work, and one piece is a normal order here.

What materials do you machine?

Mostly Ti-6Al-4V ELI, titanium Grade 5 and 17-4 stainless. If your print calls for something else, send it and we'll tell you whether it's a fit.

Do you anodize or laser mark?

Through partners we manage. We set the sequence on our purchase order, inspect the parts when they come back and reference the partner's certification on your CoC, so you still have one point of contact.

What comes with my parts?

Always: a Certificate of Conformance, a packing slip and labelled packages. When you ask for them at quote: inspection data, material and outside-process certs, and a first article report in your format.

What are your parts used for?

Bench testing, preclinical studies and design verification. Parts ship labelled non-sterile and not for clinical use.

How long does it take?

About 3–4 weeks on average, and we aim to hold every job within four. Rush jobs can ship in days, depending on the part and any outside processes.
